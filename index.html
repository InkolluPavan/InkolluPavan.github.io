About Me

Practical Data Science, ML & Analytics

I'm Venkata Pavan Inkollu, a Computer Science graduate from RISE Krishna Sai Prakasam Group of Institutions, Ongole (B.Tech CSE, CGPA 7.01), focused on turning messy datasets into clear analysis and working machine learning models.
